# Content Strategy Foundations for Editorial SEO Teams 2026

Content strategy is how an editorial team decides which URL owns a query. Without that map, two well-written drafts compete, and internal links have nowhere durable to point.

## Pillars and supporting pieces

A comprehensive pillar covers the parent topic. Supporting articles take a narrower job: a how-to, a FAQ, or a complete guide to one sub-problem. The supporting keyword is the contract that keeps those URLs from collapsing into each other.

## Refresh versus rewrite

Where Things Stand exists so a living URL can absorb new evidence without becoming a new slug. Treat updates as editorial work: change the dated paragraph, add sources, and leave the URL stable.
